About

About Vindom Games

Vindom Games is the trading card division of Particulate LLC, a New Jersey-based technology and commerce company. We specialize in singles and sealed product across major TCG brands including Pokemon, One Piece Card Game, and Lorcana, with dedicated specialty focus on Bo Jackson Battle Arena.

Since launching, we've completed over 300 sales across TCGplayer and eBay with 100% positive feedback across both platforms. We ship from New Jersey and maintain active listings across TCGplayer, eBay, and our own dedicated Bo Jackson Battle Arena storefront.

Vindom Games is powered by Particulate LLC's proprietary listing and inventory automation, which lets us keep our catalog current across multiple channels and respond to market changes quickly. Whether you're a competitive player, a collector, or a completionist — we're here to help.
