Effective Date: August 13, 2026

1. Who We Are

Vindom Games is a trading card retailer operating as a division of Particulate LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company. We sell singles and sealed product across Pokemon, One Piece Card Game, Lorcana, and Bo Jackson Battle Arena through online marketplaces including eBay, TCGplayer, and our BoBA Storefront.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and shipping address when you make a purchase or contact us.
  • Transaction Data: Purchase history, payment information (processed securely through third-party payment processors such as Stripe, eBay, and TCGplayer), and shipping details.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and general analytics collected via standard web technologies.
  • Marketplace Data: When you purchase through eBay or TCGplayer, those platforms handle transaction processing. We receive order and shipping information as necessary to fulfill your order.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Communicate about orders, shipping, and delivery
  • Respond to customer service inquiries
  • Improve our products and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Shipping carriers (USPS, UPS, FedEx) and payment processors as necessary to fulfill orders.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information. Payment processing is handled by secure third-party providers (Stripe, eBay, TCGplayer).

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill orders, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at james@particulatellc.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
